Support

Get help with importing audio, playback, effects, queues, widgets, exports, Recho Pro, or other questions about the Recho app.

Contact Recho Support

Email a description of the problem or question. If you are reporting an issue, include the device model, iOS version, Recho version, and the steps that led to the problem when possible.

Email support.recho@gmail.com

Support messages are reviewed as soon as practical. Please do not attach audio files unless Recho Support specifically asks for them.

Before Contacting Support

  • Confirm that Recho and iOS are up to date.
  • Close and reopen Recho, then try the action again.
  • For Music library imports, confirm the song is downloaded locally and is not DRM-protected or cloud-only.
  • For Files imports, confirm the selected item is a supported audio file and remains accessible on the device.
  • For purchase questions, use Recho’s restore-purchase option while signed in to the Apple Account that completed the purchase.

Privacy

Recho does not require an account and does not transmit your imported audio or app activity to the developer. Do not email private audio, purchase credentials, passwords, or Apple Account verification codes.

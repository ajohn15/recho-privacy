Privacy Policy

This notice explains how Recho handles files and app data, identifies important third-party software, and describes your responsibilities when working with audio.

Effective September 4, 2026

Privacy

Recho does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or developer-operated online services. Recho does not transmit imported audio, track information, effect settings, queue contents, usage activity, or device identifiers to the developer.

Audio files that you expressly select through the system document picker or send to Recho through an iOS sharing workflow are copied into Recho’s private on-device storage. Track metadata, waveform analysis, effect settings, presets, favorites, queue state, and app preferences are also maintained locally on the device.

Recho uses the system share sheet when you choose to export a processed MP3 file. Recho does not select the recipient or destination. A destination app, service, or storage provider chosen by you may receive the exported file and process it under its own terms and privacy practices.

Imported audio remains stored until it is permanently deleted through Recho or the app and its data are removed from the device. Tracks removed from the library remain in Recently Deleted until you restore or permanently delete them. Recho does not retain a server-side copy and therefore cannot retrieve or delete content on your behalf.

File Access & Permissions

Recho accesses only audio files that you deliberately select or share with the app. The system document picker may temporarily grant Recho access to the selected file so that it can be copied into the app’s private storage.

Recho does not request unrestricted access to your media library, Photos, Contacts, microphone, location, or advertising identifier. Background audio capability is used solely to support audio playback when Recho is not in the foreground.
